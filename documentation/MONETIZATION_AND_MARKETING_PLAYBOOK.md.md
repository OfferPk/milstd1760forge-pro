# MILSTD1760Forge Pro Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Commercial Market Landscape

MIL-STD-1760 is the mandatory digital interface standard connecting tactical combat aircraft (F-15, F-16, F/A-18, F-35, Eurofighter Typhoon, Gripen, Rafale) to smart weapons, precision guidance kits, and electronic warfare / targeting pods. Specialized defense hardware bus test sets (DDC, Ballard Technology, Astronics, AIT) cost \$35,000 to \$85,000 per workstation. MILSTD1760Forge Pro provides a modern, accessible, browser-native development, simulation, and compliance verification environment for defense aerospace engineers, weapons integration teams, and military flight test squadrons.
